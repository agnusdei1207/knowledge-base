# Knowledge Base

This workspace keeps the team knowledge, operating notes, study material, and research records in one navigable archive.

## Workspaces

| Area | Purpose | Start here |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Work | Business planning, delivery notes, operations, and engineering process. | [Work](/knowledge-base/work/) |
| Personal | Personal planning, reading, journal, and finance notes. | [Personal](/knowledge-base/personal/) |
| Study Note | Technical study material organized by subject area. | [Study Note](/knowledge-base/studynote/) |
| R&D | Research notes, experiments, architecture ideas, and prototypes. | [R&D](/knowledge-base/r-and-d/) |
| Inbox | Temporary notes before they are moved into the right workspace. | [Inbox](/knowledge-base/inbox/) |

## Operating Notes

- Use search for direct lookup.
- Use the Explorer for browsing by workspace and subject.
- Keep new notes close to the workspace where they will be maintained.
